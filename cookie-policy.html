Cookie Policy

Last updated: July 21, 2026

What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your web browser. They help websites remember your preferences and improve your browsing experience.

How We Use Cookies

Antigravity Play uses localStorage (not traditional cookies) to store your preferences, including:

• Theme preference (dark/light mode)

• Authentication session

• Liked videos

• Continue watching positions

• Emoji reaction data

Third-Party Storage

Embedded videos from third-party platforms (YouTube, Vimeo, etc.) may set their own cookies when you interact with the embedded player. We have no control over these third-party cookies.

Managing Storage

You can clear your localStorage and cookies at any time through your browser settings. Note that this will reset your preferences and logged-in status.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
