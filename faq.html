Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions

What is Antigravity Play?

Antigravity Play is a video sharing platform that lets you upload, share, and watch videos. It features a custom-built video player with modern controls, emoji reactions, and seamless playback.

How do I upload videos?

Log in to the Admin Portal and navigate to the Upload section. You can add videos from YouTube, Vimeo, Dailymotion, Streamable, and other supported platforms by pasting the video URL.

Is there a mobile app?

Antigravity Play is a progressive web application optimized for mobile browsers. The video player supports touch gestures including double-tap to seek and swipe controls.

Can I customize the video player?

Yes! The player supports multiple playback speeds, theater mode, picture-in-picture, and fullscreen. You can also toggle between dark and light themes using the theme button.

How do emoji reactions work?

While watching a video, click on any emoji in the reaction bar to add your reaction. Each reaction creates a floating animation and increments the counter. Reactions reset each time you load the page.

Is my data safe?

All data is stored locally in your browser's localStorage. We do not transmit any personal information to external servers. For more details, see our Privacy Policy.

How do I reset my password?

Visit the Forgot Password page and follow the identity verification process to set a new password.
