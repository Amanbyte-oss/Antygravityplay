Community Guidelines

Last updated: July 21, 2026

Respectful Behavior

Treat all community members with respect. Harassment, hate speech, bullying, and discriminatory language will not be tolerated. We are committed to maintaining a welcoming environment for everyone.

Content Standards

All content must comply with applicable laws and regulations. Do not upload or share content that is illegal, infringes on copyright, or contains explicit material without appropriate warnings and context.

Copyright and Fair Use

Respect intellectual property rights. Only upload content that you own or have permission to use. Unauthorized use of copyrighted material may result in content removal and account suspension. See our Copyright Policy for details.

Privacy

Do not share personal information about yourself or others. This includes addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, and financial information. Protect your privacy and the privacy of others.

Reporting Violations

If you encounter content or behavior that violates these guidelines, please report it through the Report a Bug form or contact us directly.

Consequences

Violations of these guidelines may result in content removal, temporary suspension, or permanent ban from the platform, depending on the severity and frequency of the violation.
