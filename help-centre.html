Help Centre

Guides, tutorials, and support resources

Getting Started

Welcome to Antigravity Play! To begin, simply browse the trending videos on the home page or use the search bar to find specific content. The platform is designed to be intuitive, but we have compiled some guides to help you make the most of your experience.

Video Player Guide

The custom video player offers a range of features. Click the play button or press the spacebar to toggle playback. Use the progress bar to seek, the volume slider to adjust audio, and the settings menu to change playback speed. On mobile, double-tap the left or right side of the player to rewind or forward 10 seconds.

Admin Guide

The Admin Panel provides tools for managing videos, tags, and platform settings. You can publish, edit, or delete videos, manage tags with the inline editor, and view analytics to track performance.

Troubleshooting

If a video fails to load, try refreshing the page. If emoji reactions aren't saving, check that localStorage is enabled in your browser. For persistent issues, use the Report a Bug form.
