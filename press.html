About Antigravity Play

Antigravity Play is a modern video sharing platform built with vanilla JavaScript, featuring a custom-designed video player with advanced controls, emoji reactions, and full theme support. The platform showcases what can be achieved with native web technologies without the need for heavy frameworks.
